# Context notes — wiki consolidation (2026-08-13)

## What forced the decision

The methodology doc was edited three times in one session and pushed to the GitHub-repo
wiki by hand, while the Pages mirror (`docs/wiki/reference__*.html`, generated by
`build_docs.py`) silently kept serving a two-day-old copy. Nothing caught it: the repo's
gates cover the repo, and the wiki is a separate git repository. A freshness gate now
covers the Pages side, but the underlying problem was two publishing surfaces for one
body of text.

## Why Pages won, not the wiki

The owner's first instinct was the reverse — consolidate into the wiki and link the
viewers out. Two facts decided it the other way:

1. **Gates only reach the repo.** Mirror parity, dead-link scanning, citation-link
   checking, the sitemap orphan audit and the phase gates all run inside this repo. A
   page living in the wiki repo is outside every one of them, permanently.
2. **The wiki cannot host the interactive surfaces.** No JS, so the belt viewer, starmap,
   orbit viewers, DB browser and phase boards must live on Pages regardless. Putting the
   prose on Pages too makes those links internal, which means the dead-link gate checks
   them; putting the prose in the wiki makes every one of them an unchecked external link.

Measured weight also made the move cheap: the wiki was ~800 lines across 10 pages × 2
languages, of which only `Radiation-Belts` (278 lines) was substantial, and its pages
already linked out to Pages more than 30 times. It was a front door pretending to be a
wiki.

## Structural choices

- **`docs/guide/` as a third source dir for `build_docs.py`**, rather than dropping
  overview/FAQ/installation into `docs/reference/`. Those are reader-facing orientation
  pages, not reference material, and `_ref_group()` classifies by filename — they would
  have landed in the "Reference" bucket and buried the actual references.
- **Radiation-Belts folds into `solar-system-radiation-belts.md`** instead of becoming its
  own page. Same subject, same per-body structure, and the repo doc already carries the
  citations the wiki page was summarizing. Two docs on one topic is what created this
  problem in the first place.
- **Stubs, not deletion.** Inbound links from the KSP forum, SpaceDock and bookmarks point
  at wiki URLs. A stub costs one line per page and keeps the GitHub Wiki tab useful.

## Consequence worth noticing

Gate 12 currently enforces methodology registration in **three** places (EN index, KO
mirror, wiki portal) and shallow-clones the wiki repo to do it, so it is the only gate
with a network dependency. Retiring the portal takes registration to two places and makes
the gate offline-clean. That is a real simplification, not a side effect.
